Minecraft outfits & community looks

Browse layered Minecraft outfits made by the looms community. Every look is a stack of modular clothing pieces — preview it on a 3D character, open it in Studio to remix, and export a vanilla 64×64 skin PNG that works in Minecraft Java and Bedrock.

How looks work

A look is an ordered stack of pieces: eyes, shirt, set, coat, pants, shoes, hair, face, hat. Higher pieces punch the outer Minecraft skin layer of pieces below them, so outfits layer naturally instead of fighting for pixels.
