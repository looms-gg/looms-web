Pink Hello Kitty Pajama(Original) — Minecraft pants layer

Pink Hello Kitty Pajama(Original) is a modular pants layer for Minecraft skins on looms. Stack it in Studio and export a vanilla skin PNG — free.

How to wear it

Open Pink Hello Kitty Pajama(Original) in the looms Studio, stack it with hair, shirts, coats, pants, and shoes from the community catalog, then export a ready-to-use Minecraft skin. Layers composite onto the skin's outer layer, so outfits stack cleanly.
