September 17, 2026

**Thank you for styling with looms!**


> This one is a big box. There is a whole new way to make pieces, a rebuilt Studio, a fresh pass across Explore, the wardrobe, and the tiles, and a new home for news from the team. Thank you for the ideas and bug reports that shaped so much of it. If you spot something off, or you have a suggestion, feel free to make a post in our various community submission channels.

**New**
- Say hello to the editor, looms' own skin painter. Start from a blank texture, or open one of your uploads and rework it. Save your creation as a new piece for everyone to browse, or overwrite the texture on a piece you already uploaded and keep its likes and saves.
- Pieces you uploaded now have a Paint button that takes you straight into the editor.
- Introducing Updates, our new blog at looms.gg/blog. Announcements, features, events, and community notes will live here from now on, and every post carries its own shareable link.
- The Studio is rebuilt around two panels: Collection, where every piece in your wardrobe is one click to wear, and Assembly, where you drag layers to order your stack. Body tone, hue shift, eye height, and the Classic and Slim switch all live in the same place now.
- The Studio on phones gained a dedicated Save tab to match the new layout.
- Piece pages now have a single Save button that adds the piece to your wardrobe, with a confirm step before removing it.
- Wardrobe looks now show as regular tiles and open the look page, so checking out a look no longer needs a separate popup.
- Explore search terms now ride along in the web address, so a filtered rack is easy to share or bookmark.

**Changes**
- looms is now dark-only. The light theme and the theme toggle retired with this update.
- Piece tiles got a new look: a color-coded slot badge on the artwork, a saved count with a bookmark, and a quicker add and remove flow.
- The Explore hero and the filter rail were rebuilt, with sort and category rows that move as you pick, and the piece and look counts came off the rail.
- The navigation bar is now a floating rounded bar that settles into a full-width bar as you scroll.
- Piece and look pages are tidier: the action row groups like, save, wear in Studio, share, and report, corners were trimmed, and back links were restyled.
- Piece and character renders are sharper: hero renders draw at three times the resolution, single-piece tiles frame to the garment's size so big garments read bigger, and thumbnails are lighter on memory.
- The account menu now shows your name with a "Minecraft Account" subtitle, and Profile became View Profile.
- Settings now asks you to sign in first with a friendly note instead of a bare redirect.

**Fixes**
- The notification bell now restores the exact unread count if clearing your notifications fails, instead of guessing.
- Thumbnails and page renders now re-bake themselves after a piece's texture is updated, so edits show up right away instead of a stale picture.

**From the team**
- looms is now licensed under AGPL-3.0, with credit to the MineSkin editor our paint tools grew from.
- The blog runs on hardened database rules with full audit logging, and post images only come from allowlisted hosts.
- Added a storage guard so updated files pass the same server-side checks as fresh uploads.
- Lots of rendering work under the hood: shared caches, smaller memory use, and quicker repeat visits.

We hope you enjoy the fresh updates. Keep styling, mixing, and wearing!
