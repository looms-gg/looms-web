Custom Minecraft skins. No art skills needed.

Mix and match layered clothing, hair, and accessories into custom Minecraft skins. looms is a free modular wardrobe: browse the community catalog of clothing pieces, stack outfits on a 3D character in Studio, and export a vanilla 64×64 PNG that works instantly in Minecraft Java and Bedrock.

Modular clothing layers

Pick pieces by slot — hair, hats, face accessories, shirts, coats, pants, and shoes — and stack them in any order. Higher pieces paint over lower ones on the Minecraft skin's outer layer, so outfits composite cleanly.

Free to style, export, and wear

No art skills, no ads, no paid unlocks. Save looks to your wardrobe, share them with permalinks, and export vanilla PNG skins that work in Minecraft Java and Bedrock.
