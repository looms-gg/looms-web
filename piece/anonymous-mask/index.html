Anonymous Mask — Minecraft face accessory layer

Cream smirk, black brows. Nobody knows it's you. Everybody knows it's you. Anonymous Mask is a community-made face accessory layer for Minecraft skins on looms. Add it to your wardrobe, stack it with other pieces in Studio, and export a vanilla 64×64 skin PNG — free.

How to wear it

Open Anonymous Mask in the looms Studio, stack it with hair, shirts, coats, pants, and shoes from the community catalog, then export a ready-to-use Minecraft skin. Layers composite onto the skin's outer layer, so outfits stack cleanly.
